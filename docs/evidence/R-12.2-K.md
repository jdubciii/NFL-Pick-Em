- **Rolling Lock vs Global Lock:**
  - Rolling lock evaluates lock state per-game against each game's `kickoffTime - lock_offset`.
  - Global lock locks all week games simultaneously upon kickoff of the earliest game.
- **Excluded Windows:**
  - Accurately classifies `TNF`, `MNF`, `SUNDAY_EARLY`, `SUNDAY_LATE`, `SNF`.
- **Kickoff +/- 1s Boundaries:**
  - `tMinus1s (kickoff - 1s)`: **UNLOCKED** (`isGameLocked === false`)
  - `tExact (kickoff)`: **LOCKED** (`isGameLocked === true`)
  - `tPlus1s (kickoff + 1s)`: **LOCKED** (`isGameLocked === true`)
  - With `lock_offset = 15m`: `tMinus1s (kickoff - 15m - 1s)` is unlocked; `kickoff - 15m` is locked.
